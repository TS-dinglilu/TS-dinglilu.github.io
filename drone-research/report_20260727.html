✈️ 飞行控制

作者：Alvaro J. Gaona, David Perez-Saura, Francisco J. Anguita, Pascual Campoy｜来源：arXiv cs.RO｜日期：2026-07-24 实验/工程

From Distances to Trajectories: Real-Time SDF Mapping and Distance-Accelerated Motion Planning for UAVs

👁️ 视觉感知
